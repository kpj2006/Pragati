# Demonstration data and interpretation

## Applicant profiles

- **Mukesh Chemicals Pvt. Ltd.** — fictional chemical manufacturing project in Pune, Maharashtra; medium-scale profile; red pollution-category indicator; 17 synthetic approval nodes.
- **Rahul Foods** — fictional food-processing micro-business; green category indicator; six-node focused demonstration roadmap.

Names, application IDs, department queue entries, event timestamps, certificate expiry, statuses, SLAs, processing durations, risks, schemes and approval prerequisites are synthetic. They exist to exercise interface states and do not assert actual authority requirements or eligibility.

## Status and timing

Readiness is calculated from the demonstration dependency list and local approval status array. Sequential and parallel estimates are arithmetic examples based on synthetic durations. SLA and expiry banners demonstrate alert handling only. No legal deadline, guaranteed turnaround, approval probability or statutory applicability is represented.

## Documents and AI labels

The upload control reads file name and selected metadata for a local simulated validation. It does not upload, persist, preview or transmit file bytes. The document-vault OCR copy describes an intended future capability and uses scripted states. **Agent Mode is different:** documents handed over there are sent to Google Gemini, which reads their contents and drives the agent's tool calls. Its extractions are model output: check them against the source document. The sample pack in `assets/sample-docs/` is fictional and deliberately contains defects (an unsigned fire plan, a missing boiler Form II) and dated obligations (a 20 Oct 2026 water deposit, a 15 Nov 2026 construction start).

## Human decision-making

Priority proposals, risk signals, precedents, inspection requests and knowledge review do not make binding decisions. Any real use must show authoritative sources and dates, obtain the responsible agency's validation, allow officer review, and provide appropriate correction or appeal paths.
